Overview
The Adobe University Hackathon 2026 is an innovation challenge open to engineering students across all years and branches. The theme is "Speak to Agents: The new language of Brand Visibility." The competition is designed to push the boundaries of AI, creativity, and problem-solving, focusing on how intelligent assistants and autonomous agents are changing the way we interact with technology.

What You Have to Do
To compete, you will need to complete the following stages:

Form a Team and Register: Gather a team of 2 to 3 members from your institute/university and register before August 8, 2026. While you cannot team up with students from other colleges, cross-year and cross-specialization teams are completely fine.

Round 1 - Online Assessment (August 9): Your team will need to complete a 90-minute online test in a single sitting. The assessment consists of:

15 Multiple-Choice Questions (covering Algorithms, DSA, and Programming Logic)

1 Coding Question

1 Case Study on Brand Visibility

Round 2 - Development Round (August 16 – September 6): If your team is shortlisted, you will attend a Live Launch Session where industry leaders will brief you on the problem statement. You will then have to build a solution and submit it, including your GitHub repository link and supporting documents.

Round 3 - Prototype Showcase (September 13 – September 27): In this stage, you must build a working, functional prototype of your solution. The judges will be looking for a strong user experience, smooth interactions, easy navigation, and the overall value it delivers. The team leader will submit this prototype.

Round 4 - Grand Finale: The top-qualifying teams will travel to the Adobe headquarters in Noida to present their solutions directly to Adobe's leadership. Adobe will cover all travel and accommodation expenses for the finalists.

What's in it for you?
Winning teams can win a MacBook Pro or MacBook Neo. Additionally, the top 50 participants (specifically from the 2028 batch) can earn Pre-Placement Interviews (PPIs) and a chance to intern at Adobe with a stipend of INR 1,10,000 per month.
